# General Exchange: The Story

## Act 1: The Problem

It's Tuesday morning, 2:47 AM. You're wide awake thinking about a trade.

You've been following a company for months. The fundamentals are perfect. You've run the numbers three times. You know exactly what position size makes sense given your portfolio. You're ready to execute.

Except... you can't.

The market doesn't open for another 6 hours and 43 minutes.

Your app — the one you paid $9.99 a month for — sits dormant. Your brokerage account? Locked until the opening bell. That Bitcoin you wanted to buy? Trading happens on Solana right now at a price you like, but your brokerage doesn't support blockchain. Your crypto exchange? It has terrible charts and no real news integration. Your options broker? Requires a desktop platform from 2003.

This is fragmentation.

Today, if you want to truly invest across all asset classes — stocks, bonds, commodities, crypto — you're juggling:

- A stock app
- A crypto exchange
- A commodities broker
- A bond platform
- A news feed
- A portfolio tracker
- Maybe a risk calculator

Seven pieces. Seven logins. Seven different interfaces. Seven different ecosystems controlled by seven different gatekeepers.

And they all charge you to move money _into_ their system, then charge you again to move it _out_.

---

## Act 2: The Vision

What if there was one place?

One interface. One login. One unified view of everything you own — stocks, bonds, commodities, crypto, options. Everything.

One that never closes. Not at 4 PM. Not on weekends. Not on holidays. Trading 24/7/365 because the markets never really stop. Bonds trade after hours. Bitcoin trades at 3 AM. Futures trade overnight. Why should your platform?

One where _you_ stay in control of your money. Not your broker. Not some bank. You. Non-custodial. Your keys, your assets, your vault.

One that's fast enough for professionals to build strategies but intuitive enough for a high school student to learn how markets work.

One with integrated news, not scattered across three different tabs. Real-time portfolio tracking, not spreadsheets. Risk alerts before you blow up your account, not after.

One that bridges the gap between learning (with educational simulations like our **Monte Carlo Trolley Problem** game) and doing (with real, connected trading accounts).

That's General Exchange.

---

## Act 3: How It Works

You open the app — on your desktop, on your phone, in your browser. Doesn't matter where you are or what time it is.

Your dashboard shows everything:

- Your stock positions (live bid/ask, historical chart)
- Your Bitcoin holdings (streaming Solana price feeds)
- Your bond allocation (yield curves, duration risk)
- Your commodities (Oil, wheat, coffee — whatever you're trading)
- Your options positions (Greeks, implied volatility, payoff curves)
- Everything. One screen.

You see a trade opportunity at 3 AM. A commodity price spike. A crypto arbitrage. A stock that's gapping in the after-hours market. You don't wait. You execute _right now_ through our unified execution engine.

Some trades hit Solana liquidity pools (blockchain execution, instant settlement, low fees).
Some trades route through Interactive Brokers (if you want traditional market access).
Some trades go through our integrated commodities venue.

All from one interface. All logged in one place. All visible on one dashboard.

The platform watches for risk. If your portfolio is getting concentrated in tech, it warns you. If your leverage is climbing toward a margin call, it alerts you. If there's a market-moving news event, it surfaces it to you — not buried in a news app, but contextualized to _your_ holdings.

You want to learn before you trade? We've got **educational simulations**:

- The **Monte Carlo Trolley Problem** teaches expected value and probability
- **Oil & Gas** simulator teaches commodity volatility
- **Crop Futures** teaches agricultural markets and seasonality
- **Fixed Income** teaches bond mechanics and yield curves
- **Options Playground** teaches Greeks and payoff diagrams
- **Volatility & Risk** teaches portfolio construction

You play these games, you _understand_ markets. Not just intellectually. Viscerally. Because you've lived through cycles, made decisions, seen outcomes converge to mathematical expectations.

Then you move to real trading, and you're not guessing. You're confident.

**The High School Investor**
Learning about markets for the first time. Starts with simulations (Monte Carlo, Oil & Gas). Runs portfolios. Learns the _why_ behind every decision. Then opens a small real account and trades with conviction instead of guessing.

**The College Student**
Has some capital. Wants to trade 24/7. Frustrated with Robinhood's limitations and E\*TRADE's clunky interface. Wants to learn options and commodities without paying $200/month for Bloomberg. General Exchange gives them pro-grade tools at a reasonable price.

**The Professional Trader**
Has Interactive Brokers, uses custom algorithms, needs real-time data and fast execution. Connects their account to General Exchange, gets the beautiful interface without sacrificing professional functionality. Uses our simulations to test new strategies risk-free before going live.

**The Portfolio Manager**
Needs to see across asset classes (equities, bonds, crypto, commodities) on one screen. Needs risk alerts. Needs to test portfolio rebalancing before execution. General Exchange is their unified nerve center.

## Act 6: The Product Experience

### First Login

You see a clean onboarding. "What assets do you want to trade?" Checkboxes for equities, crypto, commodities, options, fixed income. You pick what matters to you. The interface adapts.

### The Dashboard

One screen. Four regions:

- **Top**: Your total portfolio value, % gain/loss, and biggest movers
- **Left**: Your positions (stocks, crypto, bonds, commodities, options) — searchable, filterable
- **Center**: Charts. Candlesticks, line charts, heatmaps. Whatever you want to see.
- **Right**: News + alerts. Market-moving events. Risk warnings. Opportunities.

### Executing a Trade

You click "Trade" on any position. A modal opens. You see:

- Bid/ask prices in real-time (streaming updates)
- Position size calculator (helps you size correctly)
- Risk calculator (shows max loss, margin requirement)
- Slippage estimator (shows expected execution price)
- One button: "Execute"

You click it. Done. Instant settlement on blockchain, or T+0/T+1 if you're using traditional markets.

### Learning Simulations

You navigate to "Solutions" — our educational hub. You see:

- **Monte Carlo** — probabilistic decision-making
- **Oil & Gas** — commodity volatility trading
- **Crop Futures** — agricultural cycles and futures contracts
- **Fixed Income** — bond pricing, duration, convexity
- **Options Playground** — Greeks, strategies, payoff diagrams
- **Volatility & Risk** — VIX, portfolio construction, rebalancing

Each one is an interactive game. You make decisions. You see outcomes. You learn.

### Mobile

Same experience. Responsive design. Touch-optimized. Trading on the go because the market never closes.

## Act 7: The Business Model

We're not taking custody of your assets, so we don't have the regulatory burden of a bank.
We're not charging per trade, so we're cheaper than traditional brokers.
We're not taking a spread on every execution, so we're faster and more transparent.

We make money from:

- **Subscription tiers** (Free for learning, Pro for professional traders)
- **Integration fees** (when you connect Interactive Brokers, we get a small percentage)
- **Premium data** (Bloomberg-grade real-time market data for power users)
- **Lending** (collateralize crypto holdings to borrow stablecoins, we take a spread)

No hidden fees. No payment for order flow. No proprietary spreads. Transparent pricing.

## For Cursor AI: Build Instructions

Use this narrative as your north star. Every feature should serve this story.

**Tone**: Professional but human. Urgent but not panicky. Visionary but practical.

**Design**: Modern, clean, fast. Dark mode optimized (traders love dark mode). No skeuomorphism. Real buttons that look clickable. Charts that load instantly.

**Educational Simulations**: Each should have a clear learning objective. The Monte Carlo Trolley Problem teaches expected value. Oil & Gas teaches volatility. Crop Futures teaches seasonal cycles. Make each one interactive, beautiful, and impossible to misunderstand.

**Dashboard**: The heart of the product. Make it the most beautiful portfolio tracker ever built. Real-time streaming prices. Smooth animations. Instant feedback.

**Execution**: When someone clicks "Trade," make it feel instant and safe. Show them exactly what will happen before they confirm.

**Mobile**: Not an afterthought. Responsive design from day one. A trader at 2 AM on their phone should feel as confident as one at a desktop.

Build this story into every pixel. That's how you create a product people actually want to use.
